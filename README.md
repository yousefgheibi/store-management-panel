# store management panel

My Bachelor's Thesis Project: Store Management Panel
Jul 2022 – Sep 2022

A web-based system for managing warehouse operations and sales in one place:

• Product & inventory management
• Stock in/out tracking
• Invoice generation
• Ticketing system (support/issue tracking)
• Supplier & vendor management
• Customer management
• User & access management
• Reporting

Goal: Replace scattered manual processes with a centralized panel — reducing human error and improving inventory visibility.
